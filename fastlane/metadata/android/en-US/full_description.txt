Velo.Schulplaner is a modern, native Material You Expressive school planner and substitution plan viewer for Merianschule Seligenstadt (based on DSBmobile). It shows your school's daily substitution plan in a clean, fast and customizable interface.

Velo.Schulplaner is built from scratch with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

Features:

- Material You Expressive design with dynamic color and themes
- Home screen widget that shows the latest substitution plan
- Automatic background refresh of the plan
- Archive of past substitution plans
- Calendar view
- Theme picker with many accent colors
- Local webserver to share the plan on your home network
- Debug mode and offline state screens
- Supports school substitution data via the DSBmobile API
