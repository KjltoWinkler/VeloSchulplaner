Astra is a modern, native Material You Expressive replacement for the official DSBmobile app. It shows your school's daily substitution plan in a clean, fast and customizable interface.

The official DSBmobile app has not been updated in years and is not native at all. Astra is built from scratch with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

Features:

- Material You Expressive design with dynamic color and themes
- Home screen widget that shows the latest substitution plan
- Automatic background refresh of the plan
- Archive of past substitution plans
- Calendar view
- Theme picker with many accent colors
- Local webserver to share the plan on your home network
- Debug mode and offline state screens
- Supports German school substitution data via the DSBmobile API
